Travel & Stay

Getting to Crested Butte

Flying In

The closest airport is Gunnison-Crested Butte Regional Airport (GUC), about 30 minutes away. Montrose Regional Airport (MTJ) is another option, roughly 1.5–2 hours by car. Denver International Airport (DEN) has the most flight options — it's about a 4.5–5 hour drive from there.

Driving & Shuttles

If you fly into Denver, there's a shuttle bus straight to Crested Butte (about 5–6 hours) — slower than driving, but an easy way to skip the cost of a rental car. Montrose (MTJ) doesn't have public bus service, so flying there means renting a car or booking a private shuttle. Once you're here, Crested Butte is a small, walkable town, so you won't need a car to get around.
